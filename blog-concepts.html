Install the model#

Download a quantized model and place it in res://models/. Anything around 3–4B parameters runs comfortably on mid-range GPUs.

npc_dialogue.gd
extends Node

@onready var llm := $LocalLLM

func ask_npc(prompt: String) -> String:
	# Keep replies short so they fit a dialogue box
	var reply := await llm.generate(prompt, {"max_tokens": 80})
	return reply.strip_edges()

We'll use a grid-based approach: carve rooms at random, then connect them with a minimum spanning tree so every room is reachable without turning the map into a maze.

DungeonGenerator.cs
// Carve rooms first, then connect them
foreach (var room in rooms)
{
    Carve(grid, room.Bounds);
}

var edges = MinimumSpanningTree(rooms);
foreach (var edge in edges)
    CarveCorridor(grid, edge.From, edge.To);

Explainer

How Diffusion Models Generate Sprite Sheets, and Where They Break

A plain-language walkthrough of the denoising process, with the specific failures that show up in pixel art and animation frames.

AM
Alex Moreno · Technical artist, 9 years in mobile games
Technical review by Dr. Mei Okafor, ML engineer · 14 sources · Updated 02 Sep 2026

A diffusion model starts from pure noise and removes a little of it at each step, guided by your prompt.1 Nothing in that process knows what a frame is, which is why sprite sheets are harder than single images.

1"Denoising" means predicting the noise in an image and subtracting it. A typical sampler runs 20–50 of these steps.
2Ho, Jain & Abbeel, "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models," NeurIPS 2020.

Why frames drift3

3Frame drift: small changes in pose or proportions between frames that are invisible on their own but obvious in motion.
